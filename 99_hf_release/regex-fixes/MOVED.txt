The UltiMed regex fixes live in the finetuned model's repository:
https://huggingface.co/Olicorne/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-UltiMed-onnx/tree/main/regex-fixes

They are two lists of regular expressions (drug names, medical terms) that rewrite the
misspellings a parakeet model still makes on French medical vocabulary, for example
"veine la faxine" -> "venlafaxine". They were learned from the errors of the finetune and
of its base model on this dataset, so they also help models that were not finetuned.
Their README there explains how they were built and how to apply them.
The scripts that build them: https://github.com/thiswillbeyourgithub/UltiMed-ASR-FR-v1-scripts/tree/public/08_drug_asr_rules
